# Provenance and attribution

Five shell scripts in `writing-plans/scripts` were adapted from the installed Superpowers 7.0.0 package, specifically its `executing-plans/scripts` and `subagent-driven-development/scripts` directories. The original MIT license is preserved at `writing-plans/scripts/LICENSE.superpowers`.

Adaptations placed the scripts in one directory, made dependent scripts callable through Bash without executable bits, checked task numbers and task existence, and reminded reviewers to inspect uncommitted files separately. The scripts do not require Superpowers or Codex to be installed.

The Strata skill router and supporting procedure text were rewritten for this package. The security-audit procedure originated in the user-provided package and was subsequently edited into concise English with the other procedures. This notice does not claim authorship of the original Superpowers scripts.
